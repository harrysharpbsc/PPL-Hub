Air Law mock, 4 of 20 1:12

At night you see a steady red light ahead and slightly to your right, at your level. Its bearing isn't changing. What should you do?

Rules of the Air

CorrectDebrief

Red is the left wingtip light, so you are looking at its left side: it is crossing from your right. A steady bearing means you are converging. The aircraft that has the other on its right gives way, and must not pass over, under or ahead of it, so you turn right and pass behind.

The trap is B, which is what you would do if you saw its green light. C gives way but passes ahead, and a tail light is white, so D is out.
